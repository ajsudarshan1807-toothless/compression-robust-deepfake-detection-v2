Why Compression Hides Deepfake Cues

  • DCT Quantization: High-frequency coefficients in $8\times 8$ blocks are divided by large quant-table values and rounded to 0.
  • Haar Wavelet Attenuation: High-frequency subbands ($LH_2, HL_2, HH_2$) lose energy rapidly under quality factors $≤ 50$.
  • Multi-Scale Defense: Our dual-stream ensemble pairs deep semantic CLIP tokens with spatial residuals so detection stays active even when frequency cues are destroyed.
